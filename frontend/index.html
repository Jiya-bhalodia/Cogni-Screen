Why CogniScreen

🧠
Multi-Domain Assessment
Covers all major ACE-III domains — attention, memory, fluency, language, and reasoning — in a single focused session.
🎤
Real-Time Speech AI
Local Whisper model transcribes speech and analyses word count, vocabulary diversity, fluency, and repetition patterns.
🔀
Unique Every Time
Words drawn from a 100-word bank, any of 21 fluency letters, and AI-generated math questions — no two tests are identical.
📈
Longitudinal Tracking
Every result is saved. View score trends with line charts across cognitive, memory, and speech domains over time.
🔒
Runs Locally
SQLite database, local Whisper model. All data stays on your machine — nothing leaves your device unless you choose.
⏰
Delayed Recall Test
A surprise image recall test at the end tests long-term retention after a delay — a key clinical screening marker.
